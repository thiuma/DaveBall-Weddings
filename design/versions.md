2. Welcome statement with the brass ornament (L3)
3. About: text on linen + arch portrait (pxl-180407131) (L4)
4. My services: big heading + **three cards**. Layout from K12, then a UX pass:
   - Eyebrow = duration/format (15–30 MINUTES · 10 MINUTES · THREE MOMENTS), not price
   - Title (fixed 2-line slot so descriptions align) → full PDF description
   - Price at the bottom above a rule, large and in high-contrast ink ($750 big, "+HST" small)
   - Full-width "Book a free consultation →" button on each card (target ≥ 48px tall)
   - Full length ceremony is the featured card (forest-deep fill), since the PDF calls it "the full package"
   - Brass is decoration only: brass text on the paper background is 2.86:1 and fails WCAG AA
5. All services include / Other considerations, on forest green
6. Testimonial: **quote panel** (linen, inset sage border, ornament, centred quote, script signature) + full-height photo ll-783. Layout from K14. *(Updated 2026-11-16)*
